Corpus Architecture

Vectorization Model Diagnostics

Overview of the vocabulary distribution, inverse document frequencies, and dimensional parameters configured in the model.

01

Sublinear Term Frequency

Applies logarithmic scaling to prevent high-repetition words from overshadowing subtle thematic tokens:

$$\text{TF}(t, d) = 1 + \log(\text{count}(t, d))$$
02

Inverse Document Frequency

Dampens ubiquitous terms while boosting rare, highly discriminative narrative keywords across $N$ films:

$$\text{IDF}(t) = \log\left(\frac{1 + N}{1 + \text{DF}(t)}\right) + 1$$
03

L2-Normalized Cosine Metric

Calculates the angular divergence between unit-length vectors in high-dimensional feature space:

$$\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \|\mathbf{v}\|_2}$$
