How Similarity Is Found (Vector Space Model)

Every movie is transformed into a 5,000-dimensional vector. Recommendations are ranked by the angular cosine similarity $\cos(\theta) = \sum (TF_A \times TF_B \times IDF^2)$.

$$\text{TF} = 1 + \log(\text{count})$$ $$\text{IDF} = \log\left(\frac{1+N}{1+\text{DF}}\right) + 1$$ $$\text{Cosine} = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}$$
