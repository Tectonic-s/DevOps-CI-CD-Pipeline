Machine Learning Theory

How the TF-IDF Recommender Works

A comprehensive look at the mathematical architecture powering this recommendation system.

1. Term Frequency (TF)

Quantifies how frequently a term $t$ appears in movie document $d$. We apply sublinear scaling to dampen the dominance of repeated words:

$$\text{Sublinear TF}(t, d) = 1 + \log(\text{count}(t, d))$$

2. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)

Measures how rare or distinctive a term is across all $N$ movies. Frequent generic words get lower weights, while distinctive plot terms get higher weights:

$$\text{IDF}(t) = \log\left(\frac{1 + N}{1 + \text{DF}(t)}\right) + 1$$

3. Cosine Similarity

Calculates the cosine of the angle between two $L_2$-normalized TF-IDF vectors in $5000$-dimensional feature space:

$$\text{Cosine}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \|\mathbf{v}\|_2} \in [0, 1]$$
